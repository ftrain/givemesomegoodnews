In their words

Racket is a writer-owned, reader-funded, alt-weekly-style publication founded by four former City Pages editors in 2021.

We trade in the same fun and fearless journalism CP specialized in since 1979: Minnesota news, politics, music, arts, culture, civic oddities, food and drink, and theater, plus local angles galore. And we do it in a way that's actually enjoyable to read.

Because it's a super bleak time for media. Because there's nothing else like us out there. Because AI-written slop insults readers, mangles facts, and dehumanizes journalism. Because you can never have too many outlets representing workers, artists, and independents. Because the Star Tribune is owned by a billionaire and the Pioneer Press is owned by a vampiric hedge fund.

Racket is owned by four journalists who live in south Minneapolis: Jessica Armbruster, Jay Boller, Em Cassel, and Keith Harris.

Here's the big ask: We rely on memberships from readers like you to make this thing work; there are no grants or angel investors, just folks from the community who are willing to pitch in. (See where every dime went in Year 4.) It's money that goes directly to our writers, editors, photographers, and artists—not some billionaire in a boardroom.
