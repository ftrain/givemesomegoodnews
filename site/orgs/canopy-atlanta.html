For Community, With Community

Canopy Atlanta is a community journalism nonprofit founded in 2020. We collaborate with residents to tell stories about their communities—neighborhood by neighborhood—redefining who journalism is by and for.

Local communities set our reporting priorities

We learn about neighborhood priorities by reaching out to residents through listening conversations, phone calls, social media, and other engagement methods. A paid community editorial board helps us refine story topics for our Community Issues.

We pay and train residents to join the reporting process

We pay and train residents—through our Fellowship, Atlanta Documenters, and more—to report alongside experienced journalists through words, photos, audio, and more.

We share reporting with community members who need it

We make sure our journalism gets to residents through events, partnerships, printed materials, and more.

In our first four years, we worked in seven communities—West End, Forest Park, Bankhead, South DeKalb, Lakewood Heights, Norcross, and Tri-Cities. Before that, we piloted the concept in Pittsburgh.

Our work has been featured in Columbia Journalism Review, Nieman Lab, ProPublica, WABE, Saporta Report, and others.

Committed to Transparency

We believe in high standards of transparency and accountability to our communities, which is why we make information about our major funders and our tax filings easily accessible.
