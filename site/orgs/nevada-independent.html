In their words

The Nevada Independent is a statewide, reader-supported, digital-only nonprofit newsroom committed to illuminating the state’s most pressing issues, fostering insightful conversations and holding those in power to account. We tell the story of the Silver State and its people in a timely and nuanced way that promotes civic engagement and empowers Nevadans to improve their communities and quality of life.

We do not have a paywall, and all our content is free. Regular readers and supporters of our mission can choose to become sustaining members through a monthly or annual donation. We are supported by grants from a variety of charitable foundations, philanthropists who value nonpartisan journalism, corporate donations, memberships, and event ticket sales and sponsorships. We disclose all our members and donors on this searchable page as well as in stories in which they are mentioned. Donors have no influence over our content.

The Nevada Independent is the dba for Nevada News Bureau, Inc., a 501(c)3 nonprofit founded in 2010 by Elizabeth Thompson, who served as The Nevada Independent's editor-in-chief through most of 2023. You can read CEO Jon Ralston's Jan. 16, 2017 introduction of The Nevada Independent here.

Our primary focus areas are state and local government; campaigns and elections; the Nevada economy and business; and the following policy areas: education; health care; the environment; courts; immigration; and energy. We endeavor to provide accountability journalism — holding elected officials and government accountable to voters — as often as possible.

We provide content in both English and Spanish in order to serve the entire Nevada community.

We have formed content-sharing agreements with a number of Nevada's rural and small town newspapers, many of whom struggle to adequately staff their newsrooms for financial reasons. Stories written by The Nevada Independent are re-published free of charge by the following media outlets: Elko Daily Free Press, The Fernley Reporter, Winnemucca Publishing, among others. Read more about our rural partnerships here.

Our editorial policies including interview protocols, our approach to transparency, our standards for the use of anonymous sources and our guidelines for opinion-editorials can be found here.

ADVERTISING & SPONSORSHIPS

The Nevada Independent accepts display advertising in both our newsletters and on our website. The Indy reaches over 350,000 highly engaged readers each month and we accept all categories of advertising including political ads. We also offer event sponsorships for our many events across the state including IndyTalks which happen quarterly in both Northern and Southern Nevada and IndyFest, our annual two-day event held each year in the fall in Las Vegas. For more information on advertising and event partnerships, please email [email protected].

Our seasoned team of credentialed reporters, editors and veteran columnists work hard to provide Nevadans with nonpartisan coverage they can trust. We also publish occasional freelance work from talented journalists and columnists.

We are committed to a high level of reader engagement. We love to hear from our readers — including what we can and should be doing better — and often publish well-written opinion-editorials from private citizens (again, you can read our policies and guidelines for opinion pieces here).

Tax-exempt nonprofits are required to provide copies, upon request, of their three most recently filed annual information returns (IRS Form 990) and their application for tax-exemption.
