Making Michigan better through high-quality, nonpartisan journalism and engagement

Bridge Michigan exists to ensure every Michigander, regardless of income or ZIP code, has access to the trusted, nonpartisan, fact-based reporting they need to hold leaders accountable, understand complex policy and participate as informed citizens. We cover education, health, environment, business, politics and government accountability with rigorous journalism that follows the facts wherever they lead.

We are beholden to no group, ideology, or political party. Our journalists use data to separate fact from conjecture and follow the evidence wherever it leads.

Every story we publish is free to read. Access to accurate civic information is not a privilege — it is a prerequisite for democratic participation.

We cover all 83 Michigan counties. Our journalism is freely available for republication by local outlets, carrying trusted reporting into communities that need it most.

Bridge Michigan is a publication of the Center for Michigan

The Center for Michigan is the 501(c)(3) nonprofit publisher of Bridge Michigan and BridgeDetroit. Founded in 2006, the Center advances its mission of making Michigan better through high-quality, nonpartisan journalism and engagement. Bridge Michigan has served readers statewide since 2011; BridgeDetroit launched in 2020 to serve Detroit with community-engaged, locally rooted journalism.

The two newsrooms are editorially independent. The Center provides shared infrastructure, technology, production and operational support, so each newsroom can focus on what it does best. Both share same-day publishing rights to each other’s content and coordinate coverage where it benefits readers.

The Center for Michigan and its newsrooms are supported by individual donors, foundations, sponsors and advertising — not shareholders. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, every dollar we receive is invested back into our journalism and our mission. We are beholden to no group, ideology, or political party, and we are transparent about and independent of our sources of funding. We publicly disclose all donors who give $5,000 or more annually. Trust and reliability are core to our mission. We are beholden to no group, ideology, or political party, and we are transparent about and independent of our sources of funding. Our reporting is rigorous and fact-based, we are clear about our methods, and we own our mistakes promptly and transparently. If you spot an error, we want to know.

As a member of the Institute for Nonprofit News (INN), we maintain a strict firewall between journalism and funding, so no donor, sponsor or foundation influences our coverage decisions. All revenue returns to the mission of making Michigan better.
