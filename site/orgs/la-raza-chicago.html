In their words

Founded in 1970, La Raza has been in the market for more than five decades providing Chicago Latinos with the best source of information on local, national and international news.

Hispanics turn to La Raza as the most respected Spanish-language information source in Chicago. It is the #1 Spanish language publication in the city. It prides itself in being the ultimate source for information and cultural connection for Chicago Spanish speakers.

La Raza is a publication of La Raza Chicago, Inc and Impremedia Operating Company, LLC, companies part of H-Code Media/MyCode.

Director & General Manager: Jesús Del Toro

La Raza has been named for several years the Best Spanish Weekly in the USA by the National Association of Hispanic Publications (NAHP), and was selected by Editor and Publisher magazines as one of the “10 Newspapers That Do It Right” and as one of the “Media That Matters”.

We are members of the Chicago Independent Media Alliance, the National Association of Hispanic Publications, the LatidoBeat, the Latino Local News Collaborative, and the Local Media Consortium.

You can follow us on Facebook| Twitter| Google News

If you want to subscribe to our newsletters, please visit https://laraza.com/newsletter/

Our offices are located at 401 N. Michigan Ave. Suite 1200, Chicago IL 60611.

La Raza is part of the ImpreMedia family of outlets, which includes La Opinión, El Diario NY, La Raza, La Vibra, Solo Dinero, Siempre Auto, Bien Bonita, Estar Mejor and No Muy Caro, which together attract more than 35 million monthly users worldwide. Those publications rank among the most widely read and reputable and prestigious Spanish-language media in the United States, backed by a history of more than 100 years informing and supporting the Hispanic community in that country.

In addition to having been recognized multiple times by the National Association of Hispanic Publications for their journalistic work as the Best Spanish Weekly in the USA, most recently La Raza has received grants from Press Forward national, Press Forward Chicago, the Chicago Community Trust, the Field Foundation, the Lenfest Institute, the McCormick Foundation, the Google News Initiative, and the Facebook Journalism Project, among others.
